🎉 Welcome to Hemsedal - The Party Mountain! 🎉

Known as the "Scandinavian Alps," Hemsedal is where the party never stops! 🎊 With 53 epic slopes, 21 lifts, and THREE mountain peaks to shred, this is Norway's ultimate ski destination. After crushing the powder, dive into 20+ bars & restaurants, gear up at 5 ski shops, and party till sunrise! 🌅

ℹ️ Essential Information

🎿 The Resort

  • 53 slopes across 3 mountain peaks
  • 21 lifts (Totten 1,450m, Tinden 1,345m, Roni 1,225m)
  • Known as "Scandinavian Alps"
  • Norway's second-largest ski resort

🚌 Getting Around

  • Free ski bus between town and resort
  • Sentrumsløypa ski slope connects town to lifts
  • Easy walking distance in town center
  • Equipment delivery available

🛍️ Shopping & Services

  • 5 ski rental shops
  • Multiple ski service workshops
  • Town center shopping at Hemsetunet
  • Cross-country trail cafes

🍕 Dining Options

  • 20+ restaurants and cafes
  • Fine dining to casual pizza
  • On-mountain restaurants
  • Take-away options available
